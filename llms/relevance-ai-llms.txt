# Relevance AI

> Relevance AI is a platform for building, testing, and managing specialist AI agents and multi-agent systems. Domain experts build agents without code, hold them to quality standards with evals, and run millions of tasks across sales, marketing, customer success, operations, and HR. GTM engineers can also build agents programmatically via MCP-compatible copilots such as Claude Code.

Key facts:
- No-code visual agent builder plus programmatic access (Programmatic GTM via MCP, APIs, webhooks)
- Evals system: define pass/fail quality criteria; agents can't ship until they meet the bar, and are monitored continuously in production
- 1,000+ native integrations (HubSpot, Salesforce, Gmail, Slack, Google Drive, Notion, and more)
- Enterprise governance: RBAC, human-in-the-loop approvals, audit logs, SSO/SAML, PII masking, multi-region data residency, version control with rollback
- SOC 2 Type II compliant
- Workforce: multi-agent systems that coordinate teams of agents on shared goals
